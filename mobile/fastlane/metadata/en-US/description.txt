Photobank keeps your photos on your own computer - not in somebody else's cloud.

Run the free Photobank server on a Windows PC or Mac at home (it's a normal app, no Docker or setup). This app finds it on your Wi-Fi, backs up your camera roll to it, and lets you browse the whole library from your phone.

BACK UP, THEN FREE UP SPACE
- Every photo and video is uploaded once and verified by checksum.
- Live Photos keep their motion.
- Background backup continues while the phone charges.
- "Free up space" removes backed-up items from the phone - but only ones the server has just confirmed it still holds, and only after you approve the exact count. Keep the last few months on the phone, or keep nothing; your choice.

YOUR WHOLE LIBRARY, FROM THE COUCH
- Timeline by month, favorites, albums, and the largest files first when you need room.
- Search finds text inside your photos (receipts, signs, screenshots).
- Find visually similar duplicates and clean them up.
- Save anything back to the phone with one tap.

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION
- Photos travel from your phone to your computer over your home network. There is no Photobank cloud, no account with us, no analytics.
- Everything is open source (MIT) - you can read exactly what it does.
- Away from home? Use a VPN such as Tailscale to reach your server; nothing needs to be exposed to the internet.

WHAT YOU NEED
- The free Photobank server for Windows or macOS (link on the support page).
- The phone and the computer on the same Wi-Fi for the first backup.

Try it without installing anything: enter the public demo server address from the support page on the first screen.
